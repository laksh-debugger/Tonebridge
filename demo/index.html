Tonebridge demo

A short test video with six emotional lines (angry, happy, sad, excited, neutral, angry). The captions are inside the video page, so Tonebridge can read them.

  1. Start the server (python server.py) and load the extension.
  2. Click the Tonebridge icon, switch it on, and pick a language.
  3. Press play. You will hear each line with its feeling and see the emotion label on the video.
